How the system works

Book, pay, get confirmed — all online.

  1. Create an account and verify your email.
  2. Choose a room or event and your dates.
  3. Accept the quotation and pay the 50% downpayment by e-wallet.
  4. Upload your receipt. The resort verifies it and confirms your booking.
Works on phones, tablets and computers. Use an up-to-date browser and keep a working email address handy.

Guest guide

1

Create your account

Everything starts with a free guest account.

Directions

  1. Open Log in at the top right, then choose Create Account (or go to the Register page).
  2. Enter your first name, last name, email, contact number and a password of at least 6 characters.
  3. Tick I agree to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy, then press Create Account.
  4. Check your email for a 6-digit code and type it into Verify Account. Press Verify Account to finish.
No code? Look in your Spam folder, or press Resend code. Typed the wrong email? Use Use a different email.
2

Sign in (and reset a forgotten password)

Sign in whenever you want to book, pay or check a reservation.

Directions

  1. Press Log in, enter your email and password, then press Sign In.
  2. Tick Remember me only on your own device.
  3. Forgot your password? Press Forgot password?, enter your email, type the 6-digit code we send, then set a new password.
After several wrong passwords the account is locked for a few minutes. Wait, or reset your password instead.
3

Find a room or event

Use the Booking page to check what is free on your dates.

Directions

  1. Open Booking in the top menu.
  2. Choose a Booking Type: Room Stay or Event Reservation.
  3. Pick your Check-in Date and a Stay Duration: 3-Hours, 12-Hours, 24-Hours or Multi-Day (2+ nights). Optionally set your own check-in time.
  4. Press Search, open any room card to see photos, inclusions and rates, then press Book Now.
  5. For events, also choose the Event Type (Birthday, Reunion, Team Building or Others), number of guests and a preferred date.
On the date picker, dates marked Event booked, Blocked or PH holiday cannot be chosen. Staying past check-out is billed as overtime.
4

Complete your reservation details

Tell us who is coming and what you need.

Directions

  1. Check the Selected package and Booking summary.
  2. Fill in Guest information: name, email and contact number.
  3. Enter the number of guests, arrival date and time, then choose any add-ons and special requests or setup preferences.
  4. Press Confirm your reservation to receive your quotation.
Check-in is 2:00 PM and check-out is 12:00 NN unless your package says otherwise.
5

Review and accept your quotation

The quotation itemises your package, add-ons and totals.

Directions

  1. Read the package, add-ons and Total package price.
  2. Note the Downpayment due now (50% of the total) and the Balance due later.
  3. Press Accept quotation to hold your date, then Proceed to payment.
If the resort revises the quotation, the totals update and you will be notified.
6

Pay the downpayment and upload proof

Payment is made by e-wallet, then verified by our team.

Directions

  1. Open your e-wallet app (GCash or compatible), tap Scan QR and scan the resort QR code.
  2. Enter the exact downpayment shown in the payment summary and complete the payment.
  3. Save a screenshot or PDF receipt.
  4. Tick the Terms & Conditions box, choose your file under Payment Receipt File (up to 5 MB) and press Submit Proof of Payment.
If the QR is not shown yet, contact the resort for payment details before paying.
7

Track your reservation

Follow your booking from payment to confirmation.

Directions

  1. Open Status from your account menu or dashboard.
  2. Watch the stages: Pending Payment, Waiting for Verification, then Approved / Verified. If Rejected, upload a valid receipt again.
  3. Once approved, press Download Receipt.
  4. Before payment is verified you can Edit reservation, Request reschedule or Cancel booking. After your stay, use Check out now.
You also get an email and a bell notification at every step.
8

Use your dashboard

Your home base after signing in.

Directions

  1. Open Dashboard from the profile menu (your name, top right).
  2. Use the cards: Reservation, Booking, Payment, Status and Live Chat.
  3. Read replies and booking updates under Notifications (the bell icon).
  4. After a stay, leave a rating under My Reviews.
9

Update your profile and password

Keep your details current.

Directions

  1. Open the profile menu, then Settings.
  2. Upload or remove a Profile picture (up to 5 MB).
  3. Edit your name and phone, then press Save changes.
  4. To change your password, enter the current one, the new one twice, then press Update password.
10

Get help

We are happy to assist with any reservation concern.

Directions

  1. Open Contacts, choose a Subject, write your message and press Send message. Replies arrive by email and in your notifications.
  2. Or use the round Live Chat button at the bottom right of any page.
  3. You can also call +63 970 323 6544 or read the FAQs.

1

Dashboard and reports

A quick view of bookings, payments and revenue.

Directions

  1. Sign in, then open Dashboard from the sidebar.
  2. Read the cards: Total Bookings, Verified Payments, Pending payment and Awaiting verification.
  3. Under Download reports, set the dates, status and room, then export Reservation, Booking, Payment or Schedule reports as Excel, DOCX or PDF.
2

Reservations and quotations

Review every booking and send quotations.

Directions

  1. Open Reservations. Search or filter by payment status, room/event, quotation status or check-in date.
  2. Open a reservation to see its details. Press Save draft to keep edits.
  3. Press Approve & send quotation when the quote is ready. Quotation statuses: Pending Admin Review, Approved, Revised, Accepted by Client.
  4. Use Export PDF or Export DOCX for records.
3

Booking calendar

See availability and block dates.

Directions

  1. Open Calendar and use the arrows to change month.
  2. Filter by status, event type, space or payment. Switch to Heatmap for a busy-day view.
  3. Click a date to see its reservations, or press Add booking to record a manual hold.
Colors mark confirmed, pending, cancelled, check-in/out days, overlaps and PH holidays.
4

Payment verification (admin only)

Approve or reject guest receipts.

Directions

  1. Open Payment Verification.
  2. Filter by date submitted or status (Waiting for Verification, Approved, Rejected).
  3. Open the uploaded receipt, compare it with the quotation, then Approve or Reject. The guest is emailed automatically.
5

Live chat and messages

Answer guests quickly.

Directions

  1. Live Chat is a shared inbox: pick a conversation on the left and reply.
  2. Contact Messages lists form enquiries. Press Reply, or New message to client to use templates (booking follow-up, payment reminder, thank-you).
6

Site content, users and settings

Update the website without touching code.

Directions

  1. Site Content has tabs: Page Text, Photos, Packages, FAQs, Reviews, Legal Content and Our Team. Changes go live immediately. Staff can only view Packages, FAQs and Team.
  2. Users (admin only): Add User, set a role (Customer, Event Manager, Admin) and Suspend or Delete accounts.
  3. Settings (admin only): update Resort Information, upload the Payment QR (JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 3 MB) and manage reminders.
Always upload the current QR before opening bookings; guests see it on the Payment page.

Troubleshooting

I did not receive my verification code.

Check Spam, wait a minute, then press Resend code. Make sure the email address is spelled correctly.

Why can I not pick a date?

The date is already booked, blocked by the resort, or a PH holiday. Choose another date or contact us.

My receipt upload failed.

Use a file of 5 MB or less, accept the Terms & Conditions first, then try again.

My payment was rejected.

Upload a clear receipt that matches the exact downpayment amount. You will be notified once it is re-checked.

I cannot book while signed in as staff.

Staff and admin accounts are view-only for bookings. Use a guest account to make a reservation.

The page looks broken or outdated.

Refresh with Ctrl+F5 (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac), or try another browser.
